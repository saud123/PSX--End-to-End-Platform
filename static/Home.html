What's broken

PSX tooling still asks you to guess.

Most retail-facing PSX products stop at a price ticker and a "BUY" tag. The reasoning, the stop-loss, and the source of every claim are quietly left out — because producing them is harder than producing a ticker.

01 — Signals without reasoning

A ticker and a direction is not analysis.

You deserve to know which indicator fired, which headline moved sentiment, and which macro variable is in play. Not a black-box "trust me" call.

02 — Numbers without sources

Every claim should trace back.

EOD price, news headlines, quarterly filings, macro indicators — every number in a PSX Intelligence brief links back to the table, filing, or article that produced it.

03 — No horizon, no stop

A verdict needs a plan attached.

A BUY without a holding period and a stop-loss is a gamble dressed as research. Every brief closes with a verdict, a confidence, and a trading horizon.

Methodology

What we will, and won't, claim.

A confidence score that means something is worth more than a glowing one that means nothing. Three things we commit to — and one we refuse to do.

Cite everything

Every number traces to a source.

EOD prices link to the PSX Portal. Headlines link to the outlet that ran them. Quarterly figures link to the exact PDF and page. If we can't cite it, we don't print it.

Show limitations

Every brief has an "Analytical Limitations" section.

What the report could not check. What would invalidate the view. What evidence was thin. Missing data is never silently skipped — it is explicitly named.
